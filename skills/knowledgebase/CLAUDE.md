## Knowledgebase

This project keeps a committed knowledgebase in `knowledgebase/` — see [docs/knowledge-system.md](docs/knowledge-system.md). The procedure lives in the `knowledgebase` skill.

**Checkpoint — before ending any turn** that made a decision, added/changed a feature or subsystem, changed infrastructure, discovered an issue (solved or not), invalidated a recorded assumption, or established a convention (adopted X over deprecated Y, a required pattern): invoke the `knowledgebase` skill to capture and reconcile it. Report entries written, especially edits outside the current task.

Skip turns that changed nothing worth recording (see the skill's capture bar).
